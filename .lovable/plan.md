# Lively, premium Asmi status cards for iMessage

Regenerate the three 1344×512 horizontal status cards so they feel like a living update happening inside a chat thread: more premium, more motion, no branding or footer text.

## Changes from the previous set

- Remove the "asmi" wordmark entirely.
- Remove the "handled by asmi" footer line entirely.
- Keep only the status word ("Message sent", "Email sent", "Call scheduled") and the channel icon.
- Add a sense of activity: soft animated-looking radiating rings or pulsing dots around the icon, gentle diagonal energy lines, or a subtle glow halo that implies the action is in progress.
- Treat each card as a rich chat bubble surface: rounded corners, refined shadows, a hint of depth, and a cohesive warm palette (cream, amber, soft violet accent) so it pops against both light and dark iMessage threads.

## The three cards

1. **Message sent**
   - Large amber chat-bubble icon with a checkmark.
   - Soft outward-radiating rings around the icon to suggest a message just fired off.
   - Headline "Message sent" in a confident, premium sans-serif.
   - Subtle dotted "signal" trail drifting rightward.

2. **Email sent**
   - Large amber envelope icon with a checkmark.
   - A gentle swoosh/arc of small envelope silhouettes or paper-plane trails behind it, implying dispatch.
   - Headline "Email sent".

3. **Call scheduled**
   - Large amber phone handset icon with a clock badge.
   - Soft concentric rings or a calendar-grid motif radiating outward to suggest a scheduled event.
   - Headline "Call scheduled".

## Aesthetic direction

- Full-bleed composition, no blank areas.
- Premium: subtle gradients, refined icon shading, delicate motion-like ornamentation.
- Lively but not noisy: one focal motion element per card.
- Clean edges and margins; everything contained within the canvas.
